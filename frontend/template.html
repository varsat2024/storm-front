Alert eligibility

Email alerts

Get an email when the modeled outage chance for your address reaches 25%, at most once a day. We email you once to confirm; alerts start after you click the link in that email. Needs the Stormfront API running.
Your email and address are stored privately by the Stormfront API (a SQLite file), used only for these alerts, and deleted when you unsubscribe. The risk check uses the nearest mapped premise (an approximate 400 m cell), not your exact address.
